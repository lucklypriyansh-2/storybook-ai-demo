Starring Me · Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Who we are

Starring Me is a private household app built by Priyansh for his own family. It is not offered to other families yet. If you have a question about this policy, email lucklypriyansh@gmail.com.

What we store

Only what a reel needs to work:

  • a child's first or display name;
  • cartoon avatar traits — skin tone, hair, and outfit colour;
  • reel edits — the scene, outfit, co-star, and a short line of text;
  • the books generated from those reels;
  • consent choices and any Instagram drafts you create.

We never ask for or store photos of a child, birthdates, location, or biometrics.

Where it's stored

In this version (v1) everything stays on your device, in the browser's storage (IndexedDB), and reels are filmed on the device. A later version will store the same data in our Supabase database (EU/US region, encrypted at rest), with row-level security so that only your household can see it. We'll update this page before that switch.

Consent

Making reels is the only required choice. Family sharing, AI improvement, marketing, and social publishing are separate switches, each in Grown-ups behind a parent gate:

  • AI improvement is off by default, uses a separate consent, and is never used without your opt-in.
  • Marketing is off.
  • Social publishing is off until you turn it on.

You can change any of these at any time.

Publishing

The Publish button only creates a draft. Nothing is posted to Instagram or any other platform without a parent tapping Post for that specific post. Default captions contain no real name, age, or location.

No ads or tracking

No ads, no third-party trackers, no public profiles, no comments, and no chat.

AI and costs

v1 uses no paid AI services. Reels are made from our own templates and art, on your device.

Retention and deletion

You can delete everything at any time in Grown-ups → "Delete Mira's data", or email us and we will delete it within 30 days. Server-side data (in later versions) is deleted on request, and render files auto-delete after 90 days unless you save them.

Children

Starring Me is designed for children under 13 to use together with a parent. It follows COPPA principles (amended rule, 2026): separate consent for uses that are not integral to the app.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy here.
