FAQ

Questions parents ask

Will it really look like my child?

You see the painted character before paying anything, and you get 2 free redraws. If it's not right, you don't pay.

How long does printing take?

3-5 days to print, then 5-8 days shipping in the US/UK/EU. India 5-9 days.

What happens to the photos?

They're encrypted, used only to draw the character, and deleted automatically after delivery. You can delete them sooner in Settings.

What ages is it for?

Stories are written for ages 2-8, with reading levels you can choose.
